Pursuant to **Section 84 of the Evidence Act 2011**, electronic acceptance (including clicking "I Agree", checking an agreement box, or executing an electronic booking order) shall constitute a valid, legally enforceable, and binding contract under Nigerian Law.

### 1.2 Technology Platform Classification
**HANDYHUB IS A TECHNOLOGY INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER, NOT A DIRECT SERVICE CONTRACTOR OR EMPLOYMENT AGENCY.**
The Platform operates an automated digital marketplace connecting independent, third-party facility technicians, artisans, and specialized service professionals ("Service Professionals" or "Pros") with property owners, tenants, and commercial managers ("Clients").

## 2. CLIENT SERVICE AGREEMENT

### 2.1 Formation of Direct Contract
When a Client selects a Service Professional or confirms a service booking on the Platform, a **direct, legally binding contract for services** is formed exclusively between the Client and the Service Professional. HandyHub is not a party to the direct performance contract for physical work, but acts as the escrow administrator, quality assurance mediator, and technology facilitator.

### 2.2 Payment & Escrow Administration
1. **Escrow Funding:** Clients agree to deposit the calculated job fee or initial physical inspection fee into HandyHub’s designated Escrow Settlement Account operated via Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) licensed payment gateways (Paystack / Flutterwave).
2. **Disbursement Protocol:** Escrowed funds shall only be released to the Service Professional upon:
   - Explicit digital confirmation of job completion by the Client; OR
   - Expiry of the 48-hour post-completion inspection window without a dispute notice raised by the Client.
3. **Price Variations:** Any additional repairs, materials, or scope expansion requested on-site by the Client must be processed through the Platform to ensure escrow protection and warranty coverage.

### 2.3 Premises Access & Safety Duties
1. **Site Access:** The Client warrants that the Service Professional will be granted safe and timely access to the specified premises at the scheduled booking time.
2. **Utilities & Pre-existing Hazards:** The Client shall ensure basic utilities (water, electricity) required for job execution are available and must disclose any known pre-existing hazards (exposed live electrical wires, structural instability, hazardous chemical storage).

## 3. PROFESSIONAL & ARTISAN SERVICE AGREEMENT

### 3.1 Vetting, Competence & Trade Standards
Service Professionals warrant that:
1. They possess the requisite technical skill, trade qualifications, and physical tools necessary to perform accepted tasks in accordance with professional Nigerian trade standards (COREN, NEMSA, trade guild codes).
2. All information, certifications, identity documents (NIN/BVN), and background records submitted to HandyHub are authentic and uncompromised.

### 3.2 Platform Commission & Service Charges
In consideration for marketplace access, lead generation, escrow protection, insurance pool access, and customer support infrastructure, Service Professionals agree to a **Platform Service Commission of 20%** deducted automatically from gross booking revenues upon escrow settlement. Net artisan payout (80%) shall be remitted to the Professional's registered Nigerian bank account.

### 3.3 Workmanship Warranty & Re-Work Obligation
1. **14-Day Quality Guarantee:** Service Professionals warrant that all physical labor and workmanship performed shall remain free from defects for a minimum period of **fourteen (14) calendar days** following completion.
2. **Remedial Action:** If a Client reports a verified defect within the 14-day window, the Service Professional agrees to return to the site and rectify the defect at **zero additional labor charge** within 24 hours of notification.

### 3.4 Non-Solicitation & Anti-Circumvention
1. **Platform Exclusivity:** Professionals shall not solicit, offer, or accept direct off-platform cash transactions or private arrangements with Clients introduced through HandyHub.
2. **Penalties:** Any violation of anti-circumvention rules shall result in:
   - Immediate permanent account deactivation;
   - Forfeiture of all pending escrow payouts; and
   - Liquidated damages of **₦250,000** recoverable via legal action.

## 4. NIGERIA DATA PROTECTION POLICY (NDPA 2023 COMPLIANT)

### 4.1 Statutory Compliance
HandyHub complies strictly with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA 2023)** and regulations issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

### 4.2 Data Collected & Purpose
1. **Personal Identity Data:** Full name, phone number, official email, residential address, NIN, BVN (for identity validation & anti-fraud compliance).
2. **Geolocation Data:** Precise GPS coordinates collected during booking and dispatch to match nearest artisans and track job routes.
3. **Financial Telemetry:** Tokenized card references and bank account details processed securely under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance via licensed payment partners.

### 4.3 Lawful Bases for Processing
- **Contractual Performance:** To match bookings, process payments, and dispatch artisans.
- **Legal Obligation:** To satisfy CBN anti-money laundering (AML) and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) requirements.
- **Legitimate Interest:** To enhance platform security, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes.

### 4.4 Data Subject Rights
Under the NDPA 2023, Users possess the right to:
- Access, rectify, or request deletion of their personal data;
- Object to automated decision-making or direct marketing;
- Request data portability to another platform;
- Withdraw consent at any time.

## 5. CANCELLATION, ESCROW & REFUND POLICY

### 5.1 Client Cancellation Schedule
- **Cancellation > 2 Hours Before Schedule Window:** 100% full refund credited to Client wallet or original payment card.
- **Cancellation < 2 Hours Before Schedule Window (or Artisan En-Route):** **₦2,500 Late Cancellation Fee** deducted to compensate the Service Professional for transport and opportunity loss; remainder refunded.
- **Client No-Show / Denied Entry Upon Arrival:** **₦3,500 Mobilization Fee** deducted; remainder refunded.

### 5.2 Service Professional Default
If a Service Professional cancels a confirmed booking or fails to arrive within 30 minutes of the scheduled window:
- Client receives a **100% full refund**;
- Account is granted **priority auto-reassignment** with a 10% discount voucher funded by the defaulting Professional's penalty pool.

### 5.3 Escrow Refund Disbursement
All approved refunds are disbursed within **24 to 48 banking hours** via Paystack escrow integration directly to the Client's verified bank account or HandyHub In-App Wallet.

## 6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION & ESCROW POLICY

### 6.1 Statutory Framework
Disputes arising from service execution or escrow settlement shall be resolved in accordance with the **Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023 (AMA 2023)**.

### 6.2 3-Tier Escalation Protocol
1. **Tier 1: Platform Conciliation (0–48 Hours):** Upon raising a dispute via the In-App Resolution Desk, escrow funds are instantly frozen. A HandyHub Claims Officer mediates between Client and Professional to negotiate an amicable resolution or partial refund.
2. **Tier 2: Neutral Quality Audit (48–96 Hours):** If conciliation fails, HandyHub dispatches a Senior Quality Assurance Inspector to conduct a physical inspection of the site. The Inspector's technical audit report dictates escrow release or partial remediation orders.
3. **Tier 3: Binding Arbitration (Final Escalation):** If either party rejects the Tier 2 determination, the dispute shall be referred to a single Arbitrator appointed by the Chairman of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (UK - Nigeria Branch). Arbitration proceedings shall take place in Abuja FCT in the English language. The arbitral award shall be final and binding.

## 8. BALANCED LIMITATION OF LIABILITY & INDEMNITY FRAMEWORK

### 8.1 Statutory Compliance (FCCPA 2018)
In compliance with the **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 (FCCPA 2018)**, HandyHub does not claim absolute zero liability. Liability is allocated fairly based on operational roles and control.

### 8.2 Scope of HandyHub Direct Liability
HandyHub accepts direct responsibility for:
1. **Verification Diligence:** Conducting background checks, NIN validation, and trade vetting of Professionals prior to platform onboarding.
2. **Escrow Integrity:** Securing deposited Client funds in regulated settlement accounts until job completion.
3. **Platform Telemetry:** Maintaining software availability, data encryption, and operational uptime.

### 8.3 Financial Liability Cap
To the maximum extent permitted under Nigerian Law, HandyHub’s total aggregate financial liability for any claim arising out of platform usage shall be strictly capped at **the total Platform Service Commission earned by HandyHub from the specific transaction giving rise to the claim OR One Hundred Thousand Naira (₦100,000), whichever is lower**.

### 8.4 Scope of Professional Liability & Client Risk
1. **Physical Workmanship & Property:** Service Professionals assume sole, direct liability for physical property damage, personal injury, theft, or gross negligence occurring during site execution.
2. **Pre-existing Building Defects:** HandyHub and Professionals shall not be liable for loss resulting from pre-existing structural weakness, faulty plumbing mains, or illegal building modifications.

### 8.5 Mutual Indemnification
Users agree to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless HandyHub Pro Solutions Limited, its directors, officers, and employees from and against any third-party claims, liabilities, losses, or legal expenses arising from:
- User's breach of this Agreement;
- User's violation of Nigerian laws or third-party rights;
